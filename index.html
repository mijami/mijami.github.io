About Me

My research experience spans retrieval-augmented multimodal question answering, parameter-efficient model adaptation, and low-resource machine translation. Through these projects, I have become particularly interested in how AI systems can learn, generalize, and perform reliably when computational resources and training data are limited.

I aim to develop efficient, reliable, and secure AI systems through research in resource-constrained learning, knowledge transfer, and multimodal intelligence. My long-term goal is to pursue a research career and doctoral studies.

Research

  • RE-SciVQA — Retrieval-Enhanced Scientific Visual Question Answering

    Co-authored research on scientific visual question answering using a resource-efficient 4B vision-language model adapted with QLoRA. Contributed to context-aware retrieval, query expansion, multimodal model adaptation, and evaluation.
    The ongoing manuscript revision focuses on correcting numerical evaluation limitations and investigating confidence-gated answer verification to improve reliability in scientific figure question answering.

    Read manuscript (arXiv)
  • Undergraduate Thesis — Bangla–Chittagonian Machine Translation

    Contributed to a parallel dataset of more than 5,000 sentence pairs and Transformer-based modeling for translation between standard Bangla and Chittagonian regional dialect, a low-resource language variety.

    Status: Manuscript under review at Data in Brief (Elsevier).
