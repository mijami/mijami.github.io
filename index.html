About Me

I'm Muminul Islam, an AI researcher and software engineer pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University. My research experience includes retrieval-augmented scientific visual question answering, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and low-resource machine translation. I'm particularly interested in making AI models smaller, more accessible, and more reliable through techniques such as knowledge distillation and efficient model adaptation.

Alongside research, I build backend applications and enjoy competitive programming.
